## Short description (one line)
Bluff, wager and call "Liar!" in a pirate tavern dice game. Play friends online, jump into Quick Game, or take on crafty bots.

## Description
Pull up a stool, matey. Bones & Brine is pirate liar's dice: every pirate hides five dice under a cup, and you take turns wagering on how many of a face are on the whole table. Raise the wager, or call "Liar!" and lift the cups. Whoever's wrong loses a die. Lose them all and you walk the plank. The last pirate with dice claims the treasure.

- **Quick Game**: hop into a crew in seconds and play against other pirates online.
- **Play with friends**: host a ship, share the room code and play together with chat.
- **Smart bots**: four bot styles, from cautious cooks to sly bluffers, that read the table like real players.
- **Pass & Play**: up to five pirates on one device.
- **Make your pirate**: mix 3D pirates with hats, hair, clothes, cosmetics, trinkets and dice cups.
- **Dice Forge**: pick a ready-made dice kit or design your own: colours, pips, patterns and a wild-1 symbol.
- **Doubloons**: earn one for every die you make another pirate lose, then spend them in the Tavern on grog, cigars, steak, new looks and dice.
- **Daily chest and leaderboard**: open a free chest every day and climb the weekly leaderboard for most dice won.

## Controls
**Mouse / touch**
- Tap a die face and use + / − to choose your wager, then press **Wager**
- Press **Liar!** to call the last wager a bluff

**Keyboard**
- ← / → or 1–6: pick a face
- ↑ / ↓: change the count
- Enter: wager
- L: call Liar!
- S: Spot On (when the rule is on)
