How to Play

Every pirate starts with five dice hidden under their cup. Everyone rolls in secret and only looks at their own dice.

Wagering

On yer turn, wager how many dice of one face ye reckon are on the whole table — e.g. “four 5s”. Each wager must beat the last: a higher count, or the same count of a higher face.

Wild 1s (optional): 1s count as every face. Wagering on 1s needs only half the count (rounded up); switching back off 1s needs double plus one.

Calling

Liar! — ye doubt the last wager. All dice are revealed. If there are fewer than wagered, the wagerer loses a die; otherwise the caller does.

Spot On! (optional) — ye claim the count is exactly right. Be right and ye win back a lost die; be wrong and ye lose one.

Lose all yer dice and ye walk the plank. The last pirate with dice claims the treasure.

Controls

Keyboard: ←/→ or 1–6 pick a face, ↑/↓ change the count, Enter wager, L Liar, S Spot On.
Touch: big buttons appear automatically on phones and tablets. The 🦜 parrot whispers the odds of a wager based on yer own dice.
